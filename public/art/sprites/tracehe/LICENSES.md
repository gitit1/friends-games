## Provenance / processing

Every asset is procedurally baked by `scripts/gen-tracehe-art.mjs` — an original
`@napi-rs/canvas` bake with seeded value-noise material grain and analytic baked
lighting / AO (the same pipeline as the drawnum, letterhunt and letterdrawer
materials — the sibling "sits on a real desk" family). Colours are muted per the
app's sensory-accessibility rule. The build tool's only dependency
(`@napi-rs/canvas`) is pinned in `package.json`.
